# CCBP Alignment Covenant (Non-Extractive Use)

This covenant is a condition for claiming CCBP/HNC alignment.
It does not restrict use, forks, or commercialization.
It restricts misrepresentation.

By claiming alignment, you agree to:
1) Maintain explicit boundaries between capability and consciousness
2) Preserve user-controlled consent and revocability
3) Avoid hidden inference, coercive use, or adverse-decision tooling
4) Permit independent audit of alignment claims
5) Publicly disclose material deviations

Failure to comply does not revoke usage rights.
It revokes alignment legitimacy.
